URL Cleaner — Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Applies to the URL Cleaner Android app and the URL Cleaner browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

What URL Cleaner does

URL Cleaner removes tracking parameters from links on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It makes no network requests: its rules ship inside the app and the extension, and are updated only when the app or extension itself is updated.

What stays on your device

Your per-domain settings, statistics and recent-clean history are stored only on your device (in the Android app's private storage, or in your browser's local extension storage) and never leave it. You can export your settings to a file yourself; where that file goes is up to you.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions. The browser extension asks only for what its features need. If you turn on its automatic cleaning, it asks for access to all websites so it can clean page addresses and copied links. It uses that access only to remove tracking parameters, and gives it back when you turn automatic cleaning off.

No analytics, no ads, no third parties

URL Cleaner contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code, and shares nothing with third parties.
